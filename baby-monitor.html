Infant Baby Monitor

A low-cost wearable neonatal safety monitor: a sensor-embedded smart blanket paired with a wireless parent wristband over ESP-NOW, with no WiFi or cloud required.

Overview

Built solo as a personal project, based on an earlier concept from a Drexel research team. A sensor-embedded smart blanket continuously monitors rollover position, temperature, humidity, wet diaper, and heart rate, sending alerts over ESP-NOW (~1ms latency, no router or internet needed) to a parent wristband that vibrates and shows a color-coded warning.

Wiring

Alert logic

The blanket samples all four sensors once per second. If any reading crosses an abnormal threshold, the ESP32 packages the specific issue into an alert packet and broadcasts it over ESP-NOW; the wristband responds immediately with a matching color and haptic pattern rather than a generic buzz, so a caregiver can tell what's wrong without looking twice.

CAD model

Interactive viewer, drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Switch between the baby-side pod, the parent wearable, and the textile demonstrator using the tabs.
