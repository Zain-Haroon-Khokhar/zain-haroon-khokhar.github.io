About

A bit about me

I'm an engineering student with a hands-on obsession for building things that work: robots that navigate disaster rubble, rovers that monitor crop health, a fully wired electric car, and an app now used by real farmers in Pakistan. Whether it's assembling hardware, writing embedded firmware, or leading a technical team, I like taking an idea all the way from a sketch to something that actually runs.

Outside of engineering, I compete on my school's Varsity Table Tennis team as Captain and play Basketball, and I write on AI, healthcare, and agtech engineering for research journals.
